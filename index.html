MINT: A Unified Model for World-Space Camera and Hand Motion Estimationfrom Scalable Egocentric Pipeline Supervision

OVERVIEW

From ordinary video to structured world-space motion

从普通视频到结构化世界坐标运动

World-space camera and bimanual hand trajectories are useful for activity understanding, robot learning, and augmented reality, yet paired supervision is scarce. MINT addresses both sides of that bottleneck: EgoPipeline creates structured pseudo-labels from public egocentric video, and MINT learns to recover the same coupled state with one model.

世界坐标系下的相机与双手轨迹可服务于活动理解、机器人学习与增强现实，但成对监督数据十分稀缺。MINT 同时处理这一瓶颈的两端：EgoPipeline 从公开第一视角视频生成结构化伪标签，MINT 再用一个模型学习恢复相同的耦合状态。

After filtering and trajectory cleanup, the data retains 1,021.514 hours, 560,649 episodes, and 110,323,558 frames from Ego4D, EPIC-KITCHENS-100, and EgoDex.

经过筛选与轨迹清理后，数据保留 1,021.514 小时、560,649 个片段和 110,323,558 帧，来源包括 Ego4D、EPIC-KITCHENS-100 与 EgoDex。

METHOD

Label once offline. Infer with one shared model.

离线标注一次，用一个共享模型推理

EgoPipeline combines hand filtering, camera calibration, monocular depth, camera tracking, bimanual reconstruction, and trajectory cleanup. Its outputs are pseudo-label supervision, not ground truth.

EgoPipeline 组合手部筛选、相机标定、单目深度、相机跟踪、双手重建与轨迹清理。它的输出是伪标签监督，而不是真值。

MINT adapts LingBot-Map's Geometric Context Transformer. A 32-frame window is encoded into a shared spatiotemporal representation, then four heads predict camera extrinsics, field of view, two-hand MANO states, and per-frame hand presence. Explicit rigid geometry composes camera-frame hands into one sequence-level world frame.

MINT 适配 LingBot-Map 的 Geometric Context Transformer。每个 32 帧窗口被编码为共享时空表征，四个预测头分别输出相机外参、视场角、双手 MANO 状态与逐帧手部存在性，再通过显式刚体几何把相机坐标系双手组合到序列级世界坐标系中。

READ THE NUMBERS IN CONTEXT

Current results are not an architectural ceiling

当前测评结果不是方法的结构上限

These scores describe one checkpoint trained under the supervision currently available and measured against imperfect references. They should not be read as the maximum capability of the MINT architecture.

这些数字描述的是一个 checkpoint 在现有监督条件下、相对于非完美评测参考所测得的结果，不应被理解为 MINT 架构能力的上限。

01

The missing fine-tune

缺少的高精度微调

Stage 1 learned hand motion from coarse EgoPipeline pseudo-labels. Stage 2 used a small high-precision trajectory set to correct only camera motion; the hand, presence, and FOV modules stayed frozen. No high-precision camera-frame hand supervision was available for a second-stage hand fine-tune.

Stage 1 的手部监督来自 EgoPipeline 粗糙伪标签。Stage 2 只用小规模高精度轨迹数据校正相机运动，手部、存在性和 FOV 模块均被冻结；目前没有高精度相机系手部数据用于二阶段手部微调。

02

The teacher test

教师验证

HaWoR generated every camera-frame MANO label used to train this checkpoint. MINT matches or surpasses that teacher: all eight listed HOT3D metrics improve, while ARCTIC orientation and translation improve with comparable Procrustes-aligned joint error. The unified student can reproduce—and sometimes improve—the cascade it distills.

HaWoR 生成了该 checkpoint 训练所用的全部相机系 MANO 标签。MINT 已追平或超过这个教师：HOT3D 八项指标全部提升，ARCTIC 的朝向与平移更好，Procrustes 对齐关节误差相当。统一学生模型能够复现、并在部分指标上改进其蒸馏来源。

03

References are not infallible

评测参考并非绝对真值

HOT3D and ARCTIC annotations are measured references, not error-free absolute truth. Hand labels, camera calibration, temporal synchronization, and protocol choices can all introduce uncertainty. The scores characterize this checkpoint under the stated protocol; they do not isolate the model's intrinsic capacity.

HOT3D 与 ARCTIC 的标注是测量得到的评测参考，并非完全无误差的绝对真值；手部标注、相机标定、时间同步和协议选择都可能引入不确定性。因此，分数反映的是当前 checkpoint 在既定协议下的表现，不能单独等同于方法本身的能力。

The remaining gap is primarily a data problem, not evidence that the unified design has reached its limit. High-precision camera-frame hand fine-tuning is the next step; it does not require redesigning the architecture.

剩余差距首先是数据问题，并不说明统一模型设计已达到极限。 下一步应使用高精度相机系手部数据继续微调，而不是重新设计模型结构。

Evaluation protocol and metric notes评测协议与指标说明+

Official splits are made by original video ID before clipping and by participant where available. Camera trajectories are evaluated over complete sequences with SE(3)-only alignment. RPE-T measures local relative camera motion; it does not imply low global drift.

官方划分在切片前按原始视频 ID 完成，在参与者 ID 可用时也按参与者隔离。相机轨迹在完整序列上仅做 SE(3) 对齐。RPE-T 衡量局部相对相机运动，并不代表全局漂移很低。

ViDiHand trained on most of both hand benchmarks and is reference-only. The optional UKF is a separate inference filter. Benchmark implementations and aggregation logic are available in the project repository.

ViDiHand 在两个手部 benchmark 的大部分数据上训练过，因此仅作参考。可选 UKF 属于独立推理滤波。benchmark 实现与聚合逻辑均已在项目仓库公开。
