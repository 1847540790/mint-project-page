Why this matters为什么重要

Pretraining exposes MINT to a broad range of FOVs instead of one fixed camera. This is a plausible contributor to robustness across unseen egocentric intrinsics, but the distribution alone is descriptive evidence—not a causal ablation.

预训练让 MINT 接触到广泛的 FOV，而不是单一固定相机。这可能是模型适应未见第一视角内参的一个原因；但该分布属于描述性证据，并非能证明因果关系的消融实验。

Current results reflect the limits of existing data diversity and precision, not the architecture.

当前测评结果不是方法的结构上限，而是现有数据多样性和数据精度的上限。

The next step for egocentric data is to send a larger and more diverse collection of egocentric videos through the traditional EgoPipeline, producing labeled data at scale and at low cost to first solve the generalization problem. A small amount of high-precision data can then be used for fine-tuning, yielding an egocentric model that approaches the accuracy of second-stage high-precision training while retaining strong generalization.

EGO数据的下一步应该是使用更大规模，更多样的EGO视频进入传统ego管线，大批量低成本产出带标签的ego数据，先解决泛化问题，然后只需使用较少的高精度数据微调即可训练一个精度接近二阶段高精度数据且具有强大泛化能力的EGO数据模型。

Evaluation protocol and metric notes评测协议与指标说明+

Official splits are made by original video ID before clipping and by participant where available. Camera trajectories are evaluated over complete sequences with SE(3)-only alignment. RPE-T measures local relative camera motion; it does not imply low global drift.

官方划分在切片前按原始视频 ID 完成，在参与者 ID 可用时也按参与者隔离。相机轨迹在完整序列上仅做 SE(3) 对齐。RPE-T 衡量局部相对相机运动，并不代表全局漂移很低。

ViDiHand trained on most of both hand benchmarks and is reference-only. The optional UKF is a separate inference filter. Benchmark implementations and aggregation logic are available in the project repository.

ViDiHand 在两个手部 benchmark 的大部分数据上训练过，因此仅作参考。可选 UKF 属于独立推理滤波。benchmark 实现与聚合逻辑均已在项目仓库公开。
