OVERVIEW

From ordinary video to structured world-space motion

从普通视频到结构化世界坐标运动

World-space camera and bimanual hand trajectories are useful for activity understanding, robot learning, and augmented reality, yet paired supervision is scarce. MINT addresses both sides of that bottleneck: EgoPipeline creates structured pseudo-labels from public egocentric video, and MINT learns to recover the same coupled state with one model.

世界坐标系下的相机与双手轨迹可服务于活动理解、机器人学习与增强现实，但成对监督数据十分稀缺。MINT 同时处理这一瓶颈的两端：EgoPipeline 从公开第一视角视频生成结构化伪标签，MINT 再用一个模型学习恢复相同的耦合状态。

After filtering and trajectory cleanup, the data retains 1,021.514 hours, 560,649 episodes, and 110,323,558 frames from Ego4D, EPIC-KITCHENS-100, and EgoDex.

经过筛选与轨迹清理后，数据保留 1,021.514 小时、560,649 个片段和 110,323,558 帧，来源包括 Ego4D、EPIC-KITCHENS-100 与 EgoDex。

METHOD

Label once offline. Infer with one shared model.

离线标注一次，用一个共享模型推理

EgoPipeline combines hand filtering, camera calibration, monocular depth, camera tracking, bimanual reconstruction, and trajectory cleanup. Its outputs are pseudo-label supervision, not ground truth.

EgoPipeline 组合手部筛选、相机标定、单目深度、相机跟踪、双手重建与轨迹清理。它的输出是伪标签监督，而不是真值。

MINT adapts LingBot-Map's Geometric Context Transformer. A 32-frame window is encoded into a shared spatiotemporal representation, then four heads predict camera extrinsics, field of view, two-hand MANO states, and per-frame hand presence. Explicit rigid geometry composes camera-frame hands into one sequence-level world frame.

MINT 适配 LingBot-Map 的 Geometric Context Transformer。每个 32 帧窗口被编码为共享时空表征，四个预测头分别输出相机外参、视场角、双手 MANO 状态与逐帧手部存在性，再通过显式刚体几何把相机坐标系双手组合到序列级世界坐标系中。

ViDiHand is an in-domain reference in the paper and is not used to claim a zero-shot win. The optional UKF reduces hand jitter by roughly 79%, but is a separate inference filter.

ViDiHand 是论文中的域内参考，不用于宣称零样本胜出。可选的 UKF 可将手部抖动降低约 79%，但它属于额外推理滤波。

RPE-T measures local relative camera motion, not global drift. The current checkpoint reports 181.7 mm ATE on HOT3D and a 1.412 arc-length ratio on ARCTIC. Monocular scale error and long-horizon drift can accumulate when overlapping windows are fused.

RPE-T 衡量局部相对相机运动，并不代表全局无漂移。当前权重在 HOT3D 上的 ATE 为 181.7 mm，在 ARCTIC 上的轨迹弧长比为 1.412。融合重叠窗口时，单目尺度误差与长时漂移可能累积。
